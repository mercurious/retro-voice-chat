common.txt and extra.txt are word lists generated from the English Speller Database (ESDB, formerly SCOWL),
https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist, release 2026.02.25: American spellings, sizes 35 (common.txt) and 36-60
(extra.txt), lowercase words of letters only, with the database's vulgar and offensive entries left out.
Generated with:  ./scowl wl <size> A '' --regions US --nosuggest
